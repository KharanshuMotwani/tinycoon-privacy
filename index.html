TinyCoon Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

TinyCoon: PDF & Photo Compressor ("TinyCoon", "the app", "we") is an Android app that lets you compress PDFs and photos, merge PDFs, and turn images into PDFs. This policy explains what information the app handles. It is provided by Kharanshu Motwani ("the developer").

1. Your files stay on your device

All file processing happens on your phone. The PDFs and images you choose are not uploaded to our servers, because the app has no servers and no user accounts. We cannot see, store, or share your files.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or the content of your files.

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device, such as your theme choice and a list of recent results, so the app can work as expected. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

TinyCoon is free and shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from Settings in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

4. Permissions

The app uses the system file and photo pickers, so you choose exactly which files to share with it, and it does not need broad access to your storage. If you choose to take a photo inside the app (Images to PDF), it asks for camera permission, and the photo is used only to create your PDF. The app uses an internet connection only to show ads and to open links you tap.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives device-related information through the app is Google, through its advertising services described above.

6. Children's privacy

TinyCoon is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data security

Because your files are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us, they stay under your control. Files you create with the app are saved where you choose, and you are responsible for who you share them with.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the date above updated.
